How to use the designer

Grid rule: 18-mesh means 18 stitch spaces per inch. Because lines form the boundaries of spaces, 18 spaces display 19 boundary lines when both outer edges are counted.

Editing

  1. Open the editor.
  2. Select a color swatch.
  3. Click or drag over intersections to apply that color.
  4. Select Eraser to remove intersection marks.
  5. Use Reference opacity to compare the chart with the underlying artwork.

Verification

The full canvas is 13 × 15 inches, or 234 × 270 stitch spaces. Heavy lines appear every 18 spaces. The stocking design area is 5 × 7 inches, or 90 × 126 spaces, with four-inch margins.

Exporting

Export SVG creates a scalable vector paint map. Export CSV creates a row-by-row color matrix that can be reopened or processed in other software.
